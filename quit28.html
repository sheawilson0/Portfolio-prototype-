Quit28

A 28-day quit plan that runs in the browser. Pick a first day, log each smoke-free day, and open one small tip or tool a day.

Role
UX and visual design, build, film and reporting
Client
Smoking Ends Here, for Champs and NHS Cheshire and Merseyside
Team
James on the journey, Charlotte on daily tips, Connor on the logo
Timeline
August to October 2026
Built with
Next.js, Convex, Vercel, PostHog, HyperFrames, Claude Code and Codex

One day at a time, four screens

On a phone, where most people use it.

  1. 1

    Pick your first day

    The welcome shows the whole product: the 28-day calendar, day 1’s tip and three steps. One button starts it.

  2. 2

    Log the day

    One tap logs a smoke-free day. The button turns green, a tick draws in and a chime plays. Every save gets the same small moment.

  3. 3

    One small thing a day

    Each day opens one tip or tool. Day 7 asks for one thing you’re proud of.

  4. 4

    Help when a craving hits

    “Craving now?” sits in the header on every screen. It opens a three-minute timer with a breathing circle and a soft sound.

19 to 26 October

The first week

Quit28 counts what people do, not who they are. Every figure is an anonymous count per phone or browser, not per person. A logged day is someone’s own word, so none of this measures a quit.

From the welcome to day 1

  • Saw the welcome page173
  • Clicked Start55
  • Saved a start date40
  • Logged day 1 smoke-free8

Most people who pressed Start went on to save a date, 40 of 55. The big drop is earlier: two in three people who saw the welcome never pressed Start. That’s the next thing to work on.
